A Letter for You

A few words I hope you keep close

My dearest, Miss Lillac

In the little moments

I find myself thinking of you in the quiet, everyday moments—in a song I love, in a beautiful evening sky, and in all the little things I wish I could share with you.

Somehow, everything feels a little brighter when you are part of it.

What I adore about you

I love your kindness, the way your laugh makes a hard day softer, and the warmth you bring simply by being yourself.

You make ordinary days feel like something worth remembering, and I hope you know how very special you are to me.

“Wherever life takes us, one of my favorite places will always be right beside you.”

A promise, from me

I promise to keep choosing you—in the grand adventures and the wonderfully ordinary days.

Thank you for being you, for letting me know you, and for making my world feel more like home.

With all my love

With all the love my heart can hold,

Always yours,

Mr Lillac

Sealed with a kiss and a little bit of forever ♥
